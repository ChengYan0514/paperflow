Call for Nominations
Editor in Chief, Operations Research Deadline for Nominations: April 15, 2005
The second and final term of Lawrence M. Wein as editor in chief of the journal Operations Research expires on December 31, 2005. Based on recommendations from the INFORMS Publications Committee, the president of INFORMS has appointed a committee to conduct a full search for a new editor in chief. The committee intends to propose a candidate for approval by INFORMS no later than June 20, 2005.
All members of INFORMS are invited to participate in this process. The committee seeks your opinions and comments on (1) the current state of the journal, (2) recommendations for change, if any, and (3) candidates for editor in chief.
Qualifications for the editor in chief of Operations Research will include:
• a demonstrated interest in a broad range of topics in the field   
• a demonstrated record of research in the field   
• dedication and enthusiasm for Operations Research   
• previous editorial experience   
• vision of the role of scholarly publications in the electronic age   
• commitment to workload involved   
• ability to effectively and efficiently manage the editorial process
Further information about the journal can be obtained at the Operations Research home page: http://or.pubs.informs.org.
Please submit comments, nominations, and applications to the committee by April 15, 2005. When submitting an application, please include a resume and a brief statement of the candidate’s vision and plans for the journal. The information may be sent (preferably by e-mail) to:
